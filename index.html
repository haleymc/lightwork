The Work

Our clients and collaborators are talented, ambitious leaders and field builders who could use a mirror, divining rod, or intuitive collaborator.

Light Work is a creative consultancy and studio practice — blending business strategy, product design, and storytelling. Call upon Light Work — Haley Hoffman and her band of merry specialists — when you are embarking into new lands.

Modes of Engagement

Consigliere

Ideator & advisor mode. Residency and consigliere engagements offered seasonally, often on a quarterly basis. Featuring regular sessions, light async access, and a consistent rhythm, this is where most relationships begin.

Incubator

Project mode / co-founder for hire. Co-building for launches, new chapters, and builds that need a strategic co-author embedded in the work.

Bespoke

Standalone beginnings and discovery. For first encounters, Light Work alumni, and the misty time before we know what container we need, we bookend sessions: intake, an insight-gathering gap, then a longer working session with live recommendations — to ideate, inspire, and untangle.
